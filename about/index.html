From a car-wash startup on Shark Tank México to a food co-op

I studied industrial and product design at Tecnológico de Monterrey and graduated in 2018. In 2019 I co-founded Ecomush, a car-wash startup built to save water, and led its human-centered design. In March 2020 it won an investment on Shark Tank México.

That June I joined GoHomes in Mexico as lead of branding and digital content, and started designing for the web: a new brand, and landing pages built in Figma and Webflow. In December 2022 I joined Omneky, a Bay Area startup that makes ad creative with AI, as a visual designer. From September 2023 I led its web design: Figma design systems, Webflow sites and their localization.

Since March 2025 I’ve been the web designer at Community Food Co-op in Bellingham. I designed its WordPress theme and design system and shipped two plugins: one that sends the website’s form entries to a SharePoint spreadsheet (June 2026), and the bakery ordering system (November 2026).

I’m looking for a Designer or Senior Designer role in product and web design or data visualization.
