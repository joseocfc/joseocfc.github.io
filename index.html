How I work

I specify, agents build, and nothing ships without proof

I don’t write the code. I specify it, direct the agents that do, and prove it works.

  1. Specify

    Every open question gets decided one at a time, with a recommended answer. Anything an agent has to assume becomes a numbered row before it commits.

  2. Direct

    An orchestrator splits the work into lanes, each builder works on its own branch, and a fresh reviewer scores every risky change. Below 9/10, it goes back.

  3. Prove

    Test output and screenshots, never summaries. Then I walk staging on my phone, and production moves only on my word.
