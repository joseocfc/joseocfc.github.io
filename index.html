How I work

Every change ships with its evidence

The agents write the code. My job is the specification, and the evidence that the code meets it.

  1. Specify

    Every open question gets decided one at a time, with a recommended answer. Anything an agent has to assume becomes a numbered row before it commits.

  2. Direct

    An orchestrator agent splits the work into parallel jobs, each builder works on its own branch, and a fresh reviewer scores every risky change. Below 9/10, it goes back.

  3. Prove

    I read test output and screenshots, and skip the agents’ own summaries. Then I walk staging on my phone, and production moves only on my word.
