I design products and ship them with AI agents. The latest serves a food co-op’s bakery and deli counters.

Until this fall, Community Food Co-op copied every custom-cake order from an email inbox onto carbon tickets by hand, and its deli lead typed each Thanksgiving order three times. Since 1 November, customers order on their phones, and tickets, totals and pickup sheets print from the orders themselves. The first week took 45 orders. I designed it, wrote the rules the AI agents built it to, and decided when it was ready.

How I work

Every change ships with its evidence

The agents write the code. My job is the specification, and the evidence that the code meets it.

  1. Specify

    Every open question gets decided one at a time, with a recommended answer. Anything an agent has to assume becomes a numbered row before it commits.

  2. Direct

    One agent splits the work into parallel jobs, each job runs in its own copy of the code, and a fresh reviewer agent scores every risky change. Below 9/10, it goes back.

  3. Prove

    I read test output and screenshots, and skip the agents’ own summaries. Then I test a private copy of the site on my phone, and it goes live only on my word.
